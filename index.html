7-day community membership story launch template

4 real urgency levers for community offers — use at least 2
Founding member price
Price goes up permanently after launch week. First [X] members lock in the founding rate forever. Must actually raise the price after.
Cohort energy
Everyone starts together on Day 1. The founding cohort creates the culture. Rolling admissions is a different — lesser — experience.
Founding member perks
Private channel, founding badge, live onboarding call, lifetime pricing lock. Perks disappear after Day 7. Real and tangible.
Quality cap
First cohort capped at [X] members so everyone gets your personal attention during onboarding. Once full — waitlist only.
Day 1
Build curiosity — "something's been on my mind"
Curiosity
Plant the seed · make them feel seen · activate engagement · zero selling
Urgency planted today
Mention "something I'm building" — don't name it yet. The founding member framing starts here: they need to be watching this week to get access to the early pricing.
10 stories max
1
Hook — the thing you've been sitting with
"I need to be honest about something. I've been watching [your audience] for a while. And there's a pattern I can't stop thinking about." Calm, personal, no hype. Opens a loop immediately.
2
Mirror their isolation
"Most [audience type] are doing this completely alone. They [do the hard thing] in isolation. No one around them gets it. No one to [share wins with / get feedback from / stay accountable to]. Does that sound familiar?"
3
Poll — the loneliness question Poll
"Quick question: do you have people around you who are doing the same [journey/work/goal] as you?" → YES, a few / NO, mostly alone. The majority will say no — that becomes your proof of need.
4
Name the isolation cost
"Here's what that costs most people: [specific consequence 1]. [Consequence 2]. [Consequence 3]. Not because they lack talent. Because they're trying to [climb the mountain] with no one beside them."
5
The "better together" insight
"Here's something research — and my own experience — keeps confirming: [your audience] who do this alongside others [move faster / stay consistent / go further] than those who go it alone. Not slightly. Dramatically."
6
Your own experience of it
"I used to think I was the problem. That if I just had more discipline, I'd [achieve the goal]. Then I found [a person / a group / a room] of people doing the same thing. Everything changed. Not because I changed — because the environment changed."
7
Question sticker — collect their pain Interactive
"Quick question: what's the hardest part of doing [the journey] without other people around you? Be specific — I read every reply." Save every answer. This becomes your sales copy for Days 5-7.
8
Tease — something I've been building
"I've been working on something because of exactly this. Something I'm almost ready to share. I want to make sure it's right before I open it up. Keep an eye on my stories this week."
9
Founding member seed — first mention
"The people who are here this week will get something the rest won't. I'll explain more as the week goes on. For now — just keep watching."
10
Tomorrow hook
"Tomorrow I want to show you what's actually possible when [your audience] has the right people around them. Real examples. Real results. Come back."
Zero selling today. The founding member seed is a single low-pressure line — not a pitch.
Save every reply from story 7 — it becomes your Days 5-7 copy and objection handling.
